# Demo material sources

`history.md` is article text from the U.S. National Park Service, retrieved 2026-11-04. Text written by
federal employees as part of their duties is in the public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105).
Images, photo captions and photo credits on the original pages are NOT included, because some are
credited to other institutions.

1. "Four Special Spikes", Golden Spike National Historical Park, NPS (last updated July 23, 2026).
   https://www.nps.gov/gosp/learn/historyculture/four-special-spikes.htm
2. "The Archeology of Chinese Laborers Who Connected the Country", NPS (last updated July 31, 2024).
   https://www.nps.gov/articles/archeology-of-chinese-laborers-connected-country.htm

Edits: page navigation removed; HTML line breaks turned into paragraphs; section headings kept as markdown
headings; the Arizona spike's engraved lines joined into one paragraph. Sentences are otherwise verbatim,
including the original spellings ("asayers", "accomodate", "Trittle"), so quote checks match the source.
A reviewer should confirm the public-domain status before the demo video is published.
