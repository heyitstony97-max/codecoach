# Unit 4 syllabus: The First Transcontinental Railroad

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

1. Describe the four special spikes made for the completion ceremony and who provided each one.
2. Explain what happened at the Golden Spike Ceremony at Promontory Summit on May 10, 1869.
3. Trace what became of the Golden Spike, the silver spike and the laurelwood tie after the ceremony.
4. Describe the work Chinese laborers did on the Central Pacific Railroad and how they were paid.
5. Explain the record set on April 28, 1869, when crews laid 10 miles of track in one day.
6. Summarize what archeology at the worker camps reveals about the laborers' daily lives.
7. Explain how the Pony Express carried mail across the West and why it shut down.
8. Describe how the railroad affected the Native American nations whose lands it crossed.
